Yearly summary

Rainy day uses the IMD definition of 2.5 mm or more. Hot day means a high of 40 °C or more. Tap a year to see it day by day, or a highest or lowest value to open that day.

Data from Open-Meteo: ERA5 reanalysis for history, national weather models for the forecast. Historical values are estimates for an area of roughly 10 to 25 km around the point, not readings from a single station, so hill and coastal spots can differ from local records. The archive runs about 5 days behind, so the most recent days come from the latest weather analysis until the archive catches up.
